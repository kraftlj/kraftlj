# Lewis Kraft

**Founder and CEO · Scientific R&D and product-development leader · Scientific software builder**

I'm the founder and CEO of [Synergy Science Solutions](https://www.synergysciencesolutions.com), an analytical testing company supporting organizations that need rigorous chemistry and materials-characterization work. I work at the intersection of laboratory operations, analytical chemistry, quality-minded execution, and practical software—building the company around clear communication, traceable workflows, and responsive scientific service.

Before Synergy, I led R&D programs at Tempus AI and Illumina spanning molecular assay development, sequencing consumables, life-science tools, cross-functional technical teams, and invention-driven product development. I hold more than 20 patents and have published across biophysics and reaction-network chemistry.

My training is in physical chemistry: I earned my PhD at Vanderbilt University and completed postdoctoral research in George Whitesides' laboratory at Harvard University.

## What I work on

- Analytical chemistry and materials characterization
- R&D and product development for life-science tools
- Molecular assays, next-generation sequencing, and regulated laboratory workflows
- Scientific computing, statistical analysis, simulation, and laboratory software
- Technical leadership, invention, and translating research into products

## Selected open-source work

- [**FRAP-Toolbox**](https://github.com/kraftlj/FRAP-Toolbox) — Modern Python tools, a local browser app, and a command-line interface for analyzing Fluorescence Recovery After Photobleaching experiments.
- [**Autocatalytic bio-reaction networks**](https://github.com/kraftlj/autocatalytic-bio-reaction-networks) — Analysis scripts supporting the 2016 *Nature* paper, [“Autocatalytic, bistable, oscillatory networks of biologically relevant organic reactions”](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature19776).
